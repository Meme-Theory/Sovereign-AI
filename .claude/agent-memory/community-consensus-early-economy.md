## Settled Consensus (multiple experienced players agree)

1. **Minerals first** — target +50 minerals/mo surplus before specialists. Mining districts are cheapest and feed everything.
2. **Energy to break even** — don't over-invest. Just stay positive. Community unanimously says vanilla AI over-builds energy.
3. **Alloys early and continuously** — 10-15 alloys/mo by 2210, 25-30 by 2220. Even pacifists need fleet cap filled.
4. **Growth buildings are traps** — Gene Clinics cost 400 minerals for +5% growth. A Research Lab gives actual research output. Skip them.
5. **Capital upgrades are #1 priority** — extra building slot outvalues any individual building.
6. **One unity building per colony** — keeps tradition pace at ~1 per decade.

## Ethics-Specific Build Orders

- Materialist: rush research labs. Technocracy Science Directors are self-sustaining.
- Militarist: rush alloys. Fill fleet cap by 2220 for diplomatic vassalization.
- Spiritualist: unity rush via Temples. Can finish traditions significantly faster.
- Pacifist: turtle economy, strong research. Survive until optimization carries you.

## What Community Says Vanilla AI Gets Wrong

1. No planet specialization — builds everything everywhere
2. Builds amenity/housing buildings when should build production
3. Cannot check stockpiles, only income thresholds
4. No resource chain awareness (builds foundry without mineral income check)
5. Over-builds enforcers/soldiers when no crime
6. Gene Clinic spam
7. "AI is crippled in 4.3" — our opportunity window
