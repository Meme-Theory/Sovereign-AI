## Cross-Playthrough Observations

### Orbital Deposits Scale Massively
Across all saves, orbital mining deposits become the dominant mineral and energy source by mid-game. Sorann: 2,293 minerals from orbital. Suulan: 115 minerals orbital vs 45 from planet miners. This means our simulator underweights orbital income — it's not just "passive," it's the majority of basic resource income after the first 50 years.

### Converter Mineral Drain is Real
Order of Stebbnar (year 2220): metallurgists consume 40.2 minerals, artisans consume 30.2 = 70.4 total mineral drain from converters. Planet miners only produce 5.7. The empire survives on 34 minerals from orbital deposits. **Our G1 gate at minerals < 15 would absolutely fire here** — the converter drain far exceeds planet mining production. The gate is correct.

### Unity is a Major Investment
Multiple empires (Stebbnar, Suulan, Darhesh) spend heavily on unity through politicians + bureaucrats, consuming significant CG in the process. The CG drain from unity buildings confirms our G2 gate is critical.

### Trade Becomes Dominant Mid-Game
Suulan and Darhesh both show 15-37 energy from trade policy by year 2240. This is a resource source our simulator doesn't model but which significantly affects energy balance.

### Megastructure Economy Replaces Everything
By year 2500+, megastructures provide 2,000-7,000+ energy and 500-2,000 minerals with zero pop cost. This confirms the late-game gate relaxation described in gameflow_loops.md.
