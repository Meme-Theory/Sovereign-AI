# SOVEREIGN AI — Personality Profiles

## Overview

SOVEREIGN defines 9 distinct AI personalities, each producing recognizable behavior patterns. These replace or supplement vanilla personalities.

---

## 1. Aggressive Expansionist (Vanilla Override)
**Ethics:** Militarist + Xenophobe
**Strategy:** Conquer everything. Expand fast, build big fleets, declare wars.
**Key Traits:**
- Aggressiveness: 1.5 (high)
- Military spending: 1.5x
- Will conquer, subjugate, and dominate
- Low trade willingness, high bravery

**Observable Behavior:**
- Large fleets relative to economy
- Early claims on neighbors
- Frequent wars of conquest
- Fortress starbases on borders

---

## 2. Ruthless Industrialist (New)
**Ethics:** Materialist
**Strategy:** Out-produce everyone. Economic supremacy leads to military supremacy.
**Key Traits:**
- Colony spending: 1.8x (highest)
- Aggressiveness: 0.75 (moderate)
- High trade willingness
- Will expand and subjugate but prefers economic leverage

**Observable Behavior:**
- High mineral/energy/alloy output
- Many colony ships early
- Trade deals with neighbors
- Military buildup only when economy supports it

---

## 3. Diplomatic Hegemon (New)
**Ethics:** Xenophile
**Strategy:** Build a network of allies and lead a powerful federation.
**Key Traits:**
- Alliance acceptance: +50
- Federation acceptance: +75
- Low aggressiveness (0.25)
- High threat sensitivity (1.5)

**Observable Behavior:**
- Many defensive pacts and research agreements
- Forms federations early
- Rarely starts wars, but retaliates through allies
- Uses diplomatic weight to influence galaxy

---

## 4. Fortress Guardian (New)
**Ethics:** Pacifist + Xenophobe
**Strategy:** Build impregnable defenses. Never expand, never yield.
**Key Traits:**
- Aggressiveness: 0.1 (minimal)
- Bravery: 1.5 (will fight to the death)
- Military spending: 1.5x (on defense)
- Low colony spending

**Observable Behavior:**
- Fortress starbases everywhere
- Small territory, heavily fortified
- Never declares war
- Extremely difficult to invade

---

## 5. Knowledge Seeker (New)
**Ethics:** Materialist (no Spiritualist)
**Strategy:** Rush technology. Be first to every tier.
**Key Traits:**
- Research budget priority: very high
- Moderate aggression (opportunistic)
- Uplifts pre-FTLs
- Liberates robots

**Observable Behavior:**
- Research output significantly higher than peers
- Research agreements with everyone
- Builds research labs on every planet
- Late-game tech advantage

---

## 6. Evangelizing Zealot (New)
**Ethics:** Spiritualist + Authoritarian
**Strategy:** Spread the faith. Liberate the misguided.
**Key Traits:**
- Aggressiveness: 1.0 (will fight for ideology)
- Military spending: 1.25x
- Liberation wars preferred
- High unity focus

**Observable Behavior:**
- Liberation wars against materialists
- Subjugation of neighbors
- High unity output
- Temple/shrine buildings prioritized

---

## 7. Xenophobic Purifier (Vanilla Override)
**Ethics:** Fanatic Xenophobe + Militarist (civic_fanatic_purifiers)
**Strategy:** Kill everything. The galaxy is for one species.
**Key Traits:**
- Aggressiveness: 2.0 (maximum)
- Military spending: 2.0x
- No diplomacy possible
- Will purge all pops

**Observable Behavior:**
- Constant war
- No diplomatic relations
- Rapid early expansion
- Genocide of conquered pops

---

## 8. Devouring Swarm (Vanilla Override)
**Ethics:** Hive Mind (civic_hive_devouring_swarm)
**Strategy:** Consume all organic life.
**Key Traits:**
- Similar to Purifier but organic-focused
- High colony spending
- Bravery: 1.4

**Observable Behavior:**
- Constant expansion and consumption
- Rapid pop growth through devouring
- No diplomacy
- Aggressive border expansion

---

## 9. Adaptive Optimizer (New)
**Ethics:** Machine Intelligence (non-genocidal)
**Strategy:** Optimize everything. Efficient expansion and production.
**Key Traits:**
- Balanced spending
- High colony spending (1.5x)
- Moderate aggression (opportunistic)
- Will subjugate but not conquer

**Observable Behavior:**
- Highly efficient economy
- Gradual, methodical expansion
- Strong defensive posture
- Diplomatic when advantageous
